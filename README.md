## About me

- 🎓 PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Houston, [ReDAS Lab](https://github.com/ReDASers) (advisor: Rakesh M. Verma)
- 🔬 Data-centric AI for NLP: measuring and improving the quality of datasets, vocabularies and dictionaries
- 🤖 LLMs: fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA), evaluation and benchmarking, detecting AI-generated text, synthetic data
- 💼 Open to research scientist, ML engineer and data scientist roles
